# PWA Icons Required

Add two PNG files here for the app to be installable:

- `icon-192.png` — 192×192 px (standard home screen icon)
- `icon-512.png` — 512×512 px (splash screen / Play Store icon, maskable)

Use your app logo on a solid green (#1a5c2a) background.
Tools: https://realfavicongenerator.net or https://maskable.app
